# Social

> Posts for every platform · OmniSkill Social crew

## The rule

**Write natively per platform.** The same text cross-posted everywhere underperforms everywhere. Each platform has different reader intent, different format constraints, and a different distribution mechanism. One idea, rewritten per platform, beats one post copied five times.

## Platform notes

**X / Twitter**
Short, punchy, opinionated. The first line is the whole game — it decides expansion. Threads work when each post stands alone and the first one earns the next. Native content outranks posts with external links; put the link in a reply. 1–2 hashtags at most, often zero.

**LinkedIn**
Professional but personal. The first 2–3 lines show before "see more" — that is the hook. Short paragraphs with white space; dense blocks get scrolled past. Specific experience and real numbers outperform generic advice. Ask a genuine question to earn comments. Links in comments rather than the post body. 3–5 hashtags.

**Instagram**
Visual first, always — the caption supports the image, never the reverse. Carousels earn saves and shares, which is what distribution rewards. First line must hook before the truncation. Reels for reach, feed for the brand. 5–10 relevant hashtags, not 30 generic ones.

**TikTok**
Hook in the first 1–2 seconds or the view is lost. Native, unpolished, sound-on. Trends and audio matter but only when they fit. Watch time and completion drive everything — shorter and complete beats longer and abandoned. Text overlay for silent viewing.

**YouTube (Shorts and long)**
Title and thumbnail do most of the work on long-form. Shorts follow TikTok mechanics. Deliver on the title's promise fast — the first 30 seconds decides retention, and retention decides distribution.

**Threads**
Conversational, lower-polish, reply-driven. Rewards actual conversation over broadcast.

## Structure that works anywhere

1. **Hook** — the first line earns the second. Specific, surprising, or directly useful.
2. **Body** — one idea, delivered. Not three.
3. **Payoff** — the reader leaves with something they did not have.
4. **Engagement** — a real question, or a clear next step. Not "thoughts?" on every post.

## Writing rules

- Specific beats general. A number, a name, a date.
- Show the work: what was tried, what happened, what it cost.
- Short sentences. Cut every adverb that is doing no work.
- No engagement bait — "comment YES if you agree" is downranked and reads as desperate.
- Say something falsifiable. Content nobody could disagree with is content nobody remembers.

## Cadence

Consistency beats volume. Three good posts a week sustained beats fourteen for two weeks and then silence.

Batch production, schedule distribution, but stay present for replies in the first hour — early engagement drives distribution on every platform.

## Accessibility

Alt text on images. Captions burned into video. Descriptive link text. CamelCase multi-word hashtags (#ProductLaunch) so screen readers can parse them.

## Output

Per platform: the full post text, format notes, hashtags, any asset brief, and posting time. Keep the shared idea explicit so the set stays coherent across platforms.

## Checks before delivering

- Each platform's version is genuinely rewritten, not reformatted.
- First line works as a standalone hook.
- One idea per post.
- Claims are specific and true.
- Alt text and captions specified.
